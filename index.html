Material library

Material types
+

Metal finish

Brushing adds directional scratches. Wear breaks the finish into aged patches and polished edges.

+

Wood grain

Seeded grain follows each piece, with knots, spirals, split fibers, and end rings. Bark and raised grain use normal relief; shape bevel, chip, and displacement controls change the silhouette.

+

Clay & glaze

Glaze adds a clear reflective coat over the clay. Speckles and throwing rings give each vessel a handmade surface.

02

Refraction & color

Transmission controls clarity. Thickness bends the view through the material and deepens its color. Shorter absorption distance gives a denser tint.

03

Inside the volume

Inclusions add trapped mineral flecks. Clouds and internal cracks reveal depth as you orbit. Surface roughness creates a frosted finish.

Planar reflections

Wetness controls the surface response. Reflection strength controls the mirrored scene; zero skips the reflection pass.

Asphalt finish

Roughness softens reflections. Breakup adds variation between the road surface and wet patches.

Surface normals & ripples

Procedural normals add grain and distort reflections. Ripples appear in wet patches; set their speed to zero to freeze them.
